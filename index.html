✦ the person behind the art

hello, i'm
your artist

i'm a self-taught digital illustrator with a deep love for soft colour palettes, poetic compositions, and the kind of art that makes you stop scrolling and just… breathe.

my work lives in the tender space between the real and the imaginary — dreamy portraits, illustrated landscapes, and characters that feel like old friends you've never met.

i believe every piece of art carries an emotion, and my job is to make sure yours reaches the hearts it was meant for.

✿ character design ✿ brand illustration ✿ editorial art ✿ concept art ✿ surface pattern ✿ webtoon & comics
what drives my practice

the values woven
into my work

🌸
Emotion First
every illustration begins with a feeling. before colour, before form — i ask: what should this make you feel? the answer guides every single decision.
✦
Intentional Detail
the small things are never small. a blush of colour, a tilted composition, a carefully placed shadow — these are the things that transform good art into unforgettable art.
🌿
Collaborative Spirit
your vision is the seed; my craft is the sunlight. i believe the most beautiful work emerges when client and artist truly listen to each other.
🎨
Continuous Growth
i'm always learning, always evolving. each project teaches me something new, and i bring that freshness to every commission that comes after.
